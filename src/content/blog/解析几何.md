### 曲线通式

$$
Ax^2+By^2+Cxy+Dx+Ey+F=0
$$
<!-- more -->
[![v3qjr8.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2022/08/10/v3qjr8.jpg)](https://imgtu.com/i/v3qjr8)

记$\Delta=B^2-4AC$,则
$$
\begin{cases}
\Delta < 0  \  \text{椭圆} \\
\Delta = 0  \  \text{抛物线} \\
\Delta > 0  \  \text{双曲线} 
\end{cases}
$$
